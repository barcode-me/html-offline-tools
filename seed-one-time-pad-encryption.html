Seed one-time pad

Encrypt a 12-word English BIP39 seed phrase into two 132-bit shares, or combine your shares to restore the words.

Everything happens locally in this page. The page makes no network requests and does not persist your phrase or outputs. Downloads are saved only when you choose.

Use only on a trusted, air-gapped device. Malware on the device could read and leak your content or one-time pad.

Enter exactly 33 hexadecimal characters (0–9, a–f). Spaces and line breaks are accepted. Preserve leading zeros. The odd length is intentional: 33 HEX digits represent 132 bits. The final digit stands alone; do not add a padding digit. Use an independently generated random pad and never reuse it; a predictable pad does not securely protect your seed.

Available when all 12 words are in the BIP39 list but the checksum is wrong. Preserves all 128 entropy bits, including the last word’s first 7 bits, and recalculates only its 4 checksum bits. A valid checksum does not confirm this is your original wallet phrase. An unknown or missing last word cannot be uniquely repaired.

Each word is its zero-based BIP39 index in 11 bits, most significant bit first. The 12 indices form 128 entropy bits plus 4 checksum bits. XOR with a fresh pad produces 132 encrypted bits. Each output is exactly 33 HEX characters, including leading zeros (33 ASCII bytes in a text file; 16½ bytes of binary information).

Recovery: XOR the two 33-digit HEX values, retain all 132 bits, split from the left into twelve 11-bit indices, and look up each index in the English BIP39 list. The existing text decryption page does not support this format. This backs up the words only; any BIP39 passphrase is separate. XOR provides no authentication.

Automatically generated pads use crypto.getRandomValues(), your browser’s cryptographically secure random generator. Never reuse a pad.
