Offline encryption utility

One-time pad

Encrypt text with a fresh random pad, or combine encrypted content and its matching pad to recover the text.

Everything happens locally in this page. Your plaintext, ciphertext, and pad are never stored or sent over the network.

Use only on a trusted, air-gapped device. Malware on the device could read and leak your content or one-time pad.
