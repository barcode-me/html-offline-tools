NFC card reader & writer

Read or write text and binary data on NDEF-compatible NFC cards and tags. Processing stays in this page; nothing is uploaded or saved by the app.

!

Android phone required for NFC

Use Chrome on an NFC-enabled Android phone and open this page over HTTPS or localhost for both reading and writing. Desktop browsers and iPhone/iPad browsers are not supported.

Turn on NFC, keep your phone unlocked, and keep this page visible. Only NDEF-compatible cards and tags are supported.

How to open this page over HTTPS or locally on Android

Option 1: GitHub Pages (HTTPS)

Open the hosted NFC page on GitHub Pages in Chrome on your Android phone.

Option 2: Termux (local, no HTTPS certificate needed)

Install Termux on your Android phone and save nfc-card-reader-writer.html in the phone’s Download folder. Run these commands in Termux, granting storage access when prompted:

pkg install python
termux-setup-storage
cd ~/storage/downloads
python -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1

Leave the server running, switch to Chrome on the same phone, and open http://localhost:8000/nfc-card-reader-writer.html.

Browsers treat localhost as a secure context even over HTTP. After installing Termux and Python and saving the HTML file, this approach works without an internet connection. Keep NFC enabled. To stop the server, press Ctrl+C in Termux.

Card operation
Input format
Hex accepts pairs such as 00 FF 80 or 00ff80. Spaces and line breaks are ignored. Text whitespace is preserved.

This cannot be undone. The content can still be read, but cannot be changed or erased. Keep the same card against your phone until writing and locking finish.

Writing replaces the tag’s existing NDEF message. The tag must be writable and have enough space for the payload plus NDEF overhead.

Uses the same WENC v2 format as secure-file-encryption.html: AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 iterations.

Ready.

Card contents

Plain-text records are scanned one line at a time: use Label:password for passwords or otpauth://totp/ URIs for live codes. Passwords and full decoded text start hidden. Codes use your device clock. Binary records remain hexadecimal.

Approach the NFC card or tag

Hold the back of your phone close to the NFC card or tag. Keep it there until the operation finishes.

Card written successfully

You can move the card away from your phone now.

NFC operation failed
